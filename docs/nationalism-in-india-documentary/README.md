# Nationalism in India — 2-Minute Documentary Sample

An editable production plan for a Class 10 CBSE Social Science documentary on **Nationalism in India**.

## Creative brief
- **Format:** 16:9, target runtime 2:00
- **Style:** cinematic illustrated historical explainer; archival-paper texture, restrained navy/cream palette with saffron and green accents
- **On-screen language:** Hinglish
- **Voice-over:** none
- **Music:** soft instrumental documentary bed; no vocals
- **Editing:** slow push-ins, parallax, animated map routes, match cuts, subtle film grain, clean titles and restrained transitions

## Story beats
1. **00:00–00:10 — Opening:** Nationalism in India, 1857–1947
2. **00:10–00:25 — World War I:** wartime pressures and rising expectations
3. **00:25–00:40 — 1919:** Rowlatt Act and Jallianwala Bagh (13 April 1919)
4. **00:40–00:55 — Non-Cooperation:** launch in 1920; withdrawn after Chauri Chaura in 1922
5. **00:55–01:10 — Purna Swaraj:** Lahore Congress, December 1929; 26 January 1930 observed as Independence Day
6. **01:10–01:25 — Salt March:** began 12 March and reached Dandi on 6 April 1930
7. **01:25–01:45 — Civil Disobedience:** spread of protest and participation across regions
8. **01:45–02:00 — Recap:** connected struggles, diverse participation, independence in 1947

## Production pipeline
- Use the editable Figma storyboard as the shot/layout reference.
- Reuse or adapt the existing Canva Nationalism in India presentation for titles and supporting graphics.
- Generate or source historically appropriate visuals; avoid presenting AI-generated scenes as authentic archival photographs.
- Assemble clips in a video editor, add subtle motion, music and sound design, then export H.264 MP4 at 1920×1080, 25 or 30 fps.
- Review dates, text legibility, transitions, music levels and the final runtime before delivery.
